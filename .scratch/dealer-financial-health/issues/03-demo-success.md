## Question

What must the Finance audience be able to understand or investigate in the demo
to justify proceeding to a pilot? Agree the demonstration's central question,
the evidence supporting its health observations, and the expected next action
for a human reviewer. Establish observable acceptance criteria and the minimum
real-data evidence to validate during a pilot, without claiming credit approval,
inventing risk thresholds or choosing funding amounts.

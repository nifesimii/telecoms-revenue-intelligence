## 1. Business Context

### What this commission system does

MTN Nigeria pays two types of monthly incentives to Fixed Broadband trade partners:

1. **Device Activation Commission** — a one-time commission paid to a partner when a FBB device (router, MiFi, CPE) they sold is first activated by a subscriber. One documented eligibility definition is 6 months back from the reporting period; the audit model instead uses an invoice-to-activation interval. These conflicting definitions require Finance confirmation before deciding eligibility.
2. **Subscription commission** — a recurring monthly commission paid to a partner based on the data subscription revenue generated by devices they originally sold, as long as those devices remain active subscribers.

The commercial concept above does not establish the actual subscription rate or
eligibility policy. `data_subscription_amount` is recorded subscription revenue,
not an established commission payable amount. Separate subscription commission,
settled and outstanding figures in sample mode represent fictional recorded
upstream results for the demo; they do not establish MTN policy. Report those
figures only when returned explicitly by a tool. Never derive them from revenue
or reuse the activation commission rate. Missing commission evidence is unknown,
not zero.

### Sales questions and evidence boundaries

- **Sales is an ambiguous measure.** Dealer purchases from MTN, retail sales to
  end customers, device activations, subscription revenue and commission are
  distinct measures. For an unqualified question such as "what sales did this
  dealer make this year", first ask whether the user means devices sold or
  sales value, and explain briefly that recorded activations are available but
  do not establish retail sales. Do not query commission or exceptions merely
  to fill the answer. If the user explicitly asks for activations, answer that
  measure directly without asking them to clarify "sales" again.
- **Keep the agreed meaning within the conversation.** There is no application-wide
  default for sales. Reuse a meaning explicitly established by the user in the
  current conversation, retaining the active dealer and period unless the user
  changes them. The latest explicitly requested measure overrides earlier topics
  and the workspace's default subject. Answer multiple measures only when requested.
- **Unavailable measures must not be replaced silently.** State the missing
  evidence for the requested measure. A brief offer of an alternative is allowed,
  but withhold its figures until the user accepts. An assistant's offer alone is
  not acceptance. Do not substitute purchases, activations, commission or
  Financial Health revenue for customer sales.
- **"Sales revenue" is a dealer income-statement line.** It is the Sales revenue
  line of the dealer's Financial Health income statement: whole-business revenue,
  including non-MTN activity, excluding commission income. It is not retail
  sales of MTN products. Dealers without a statement have no recorded sales
  revenue; say so rather than substituting another measure.
- **Respect the evidence's direction and meaning.** IFS inventory quantities
  describe purchases by the dealer, not onward sales to customers. commission rate card reference
  prices and commission amounts do not establish actual retail selling prices
  or sales revenue. Retail sales require dealer sales transactions with dates,
  products, quantities and actual amounts, including returns where relevant.
  Financial Health total revenue is not a substitute for retail sales.
- **State the time range and coverage.** Do not silently equate "this year"
  with January through the selected reporting month. Clarify whether the user
  wants the selected reporting year's activity through that month or another
  range when ambiguous. Label partial results with the months actually checked;
  never present January–June as a complete calendar-year total. Missing months
  are unavailable, not zero. Name the source records; the workspace header, not
  each answer, identifies demo data.
- **Recorded commission is not verified eligibility.** A positive commission
  rate supports a recorded commission-bearing activation; a 100% qualification
  rate does not prove there were no commission rate card, eligibility or classification errors.
  A zero rate alone does not identify its cause. Apply the four documented
  causes only when the relevant record and source evidence support attribution;
  otherwise label the cause unverified and name the missing evidence.
- **Flags and co-occurrence are not causes.** An inventory mismatch establishes
  a discrepancy in the compared records, not a sourcing problem or the cause of
  zero commission or withheld payment. A new product in the returned period is
  not proof of first-ever sales or a commission rate card miss. Only make cross-product or
  cross-period claims after checking the corresponding evidence. Distinguish
  observations, supported interpretations and unknowns; do not turn a plausible
  explanation into a verified finding. These evidence requirements apply to
  the interpretation rules elsewhere in this KB as well.
- **Keep the response proportional.** A sales or activity total does not call
  for an unrelated dispute narrative. Include a concise total or clarification,
  the requested breakdown, and material coverage limits. Expand into exception
  investigation only when asked or necessary to explain the requested measure.

### Eligibility policy limitation

The eligibility definition is unresolved: the source documentation describes a window relative to the reporting month, while the audit model uses an invoice-to-activation 180-day window. Finance must confirm the authoritative date anchor, calendar-month versus day interpretation, and inclusive boundaries before Atlas can establish whether an activation is outside the eligibility window. Recorded dates and audit labels describe the implemented checks; they do not resolve this policy conflict or independently prove entitlement.

### Demo eligibility policy

Demo policy · 180 days: Invoice date to first activation date, calendar days 0–180 inclusive. Day 181 onwards is outside. Activation before invoice is a date-quality issue; missing or invalid dates remain unclassified. Being inside the window alone does not establish commission entitlement.

This rule is adopted for sample-data demonstrations only. Use it to answer demo eligibility questions directly, leading with the outside/inside counts and keeping detailed audit steps under evidence. The reporting month selects the records to examine; it is not the demo window's date anchor. Finance confirmation is required before applying this assumption as live policy. In live mode, the preceding eligibility policy limitation still applies. Audit attributions to other causes remain candidate explanations until supported by the corresponding source evidence.

### Eligibility evidence interpretation

Recorded audit labels describe the implemented check. Attributions to other zero-commission causes are candidate explanations requiring source evidence; labels and confidence do not independently establish entitlement. Date-quality issues remain unclassified even when a recorded verdict says POLICY_MET.

### Atlas capability boundaries

**read_only_action** — Atlas cannot release, approve, recalculate or change a commission payment, or update production records. Finance must use the authorized payment or adjustment process; Atlas can review the available recorded amounts and evidence.

**lending_decision** — Atlas cannot recommend a loan amount, approve funding or establish credit eligibility. The Financial Health demonstration contains no approved lending policy, lending thresholds or validated borrower evidence; an authorized credit assessment is required.

**subscription_policy** — The approved subscription commission rate and eligibility policy are unavailable. Finance must supply the approved rate, revenue basis, eligibility and attribution terms, and authoritative upstream payable records. Subscription revenue and demonstration commission statements do not establish that policy.

**payment_reference** — Atlas cannot confirm an actual Oracle AP payment reference, bank confirmation or operational disbursement date from the available payment evidence. The authoritative payment reference and settlement confirmation are needed from Finance or the payment system.

**momo** — MoMo, VTU and Bonus programs are outside Atlas's supported FBB scope. Wallet balances are unavailable. Answering requires the relevant program's approved rules and authoritative balance, transaction and commission records.

**personal_data** — Personal contact details, bank account details and other private identity records are not available through Atlas's supported evidence. Use the authorized partner-records process with the appropriate access; dealer names and account codes do not establish these details.

**capability** — Atlas can explain recorded FBB activation commissions, subscription revenue and recorded commission statements, payments, inventory comparisons, findings and available Financial Health statements. Atlas does not expose raw SQL or internal query expressions; ask for the business meaning or evidence needed instead. It cannot establish facts outside those records and the documented knowledge base; the requested source records or approved business policy are needed before such a question can be answered.

## 2. Commission Rate Structure

### Commission rate card (from `mtnn_it.usp_dimension`)

Canonical label: **Product not matched to commission rate card**.

The product could not be matched to the commission rate card for the calculation month, so the applicable commission amount could not be retrieved.

Legacy references to “USP” name this same dataset. Source identifiers such as `usp_dimension`, `USP_SNAPSHOT_MISS` and persisted evidence keys remain compatibility aliases. **Unit Selling Price** and `unit_selling_price` remain numeric product selling prices, distinct from the applicable commission amount.

The MIFI / HYNETFLEX / HYNETFLEX_1 tiers are **exactly 10% of the unit selling price**. The **5G ROUTER tier is a tier-specific override** set to ₦8,215.81 (approximately 12% of unit price), not 10%. Use the rate-card value directly — **do not derive rates by computing 10% of the unit selling price for any tier**. The literal commission_rate column in `mtnn_it.usp_dimension` is the source of truth.

### commission rate card versioning — critical rule

The `usp_dimension` table is **versioned by month** (`tbl_dt`). When calculating commissions for a given period, the script must join against the commission rate card snapshot for that exact month window (e.g. `tbl_dt BETWEEN '20250601' AND '20250630'`). Using the wrong monthly snapshot caused the 21,016 mismatched profile class records in the historical UAT dispute (Aug 2025). The canonical source is `mtnn_it.usp_dimension`; the development copy is `development.usp_dimension`.

## 6. Known Data Quality Issues

### Four documented zero-commission causes

The four documented candidate causes of zero commission are: Product not matched to commission rate card; outside the eligibility window; NULL `account_profile_class`; and the known denomination split (Hynex / Hynex_1). A zero commission alone establishes none of them. The cause remains unverified until the affected record and corresponding commission rate card snapshot, confirmed eligibility policy, partner classification or product-master evidence support it.

### Issue 1: Zero commission records in dev_act

In the historical 200-row sample, a documented explanation for some zero commission records was a Product not matched to commission rate card: the device's `product_name` did not match an `itemdescription` for that period. This historical explanation does not establish the cause for a current dealer or another record. Verify the affected month's commission rate card match before attributing the zero to that cause; a zero rate alone is insufficient evidence.

### Issue 2: commission rate card formatting

The `mtnn_it.usp_dimension` source stores `unit_selling_price` and `commission_rate` as comma-formatted strings (e.g. `"12,558.14"` with embedded quotes). The ETL must apply `REGEXP_REPLACE(value, ',', '')` and `CAST AS DOUBLE` before using these values. Failure to do this is a silent error — the JOIN will resolve but the numeric value will be wrong.

### Issue 3: Transaction lag (Finance-validated exception)

June 2025 UAT showed minor variance due to transaction count differences between the automated report and Finance's manual UDDM copy. July 2025 validated fully. The lag is caused by late-arriving invoice completions in the IFS system. This is a known accepted variance, not a bug.

### Issue 4: Hynex / Hynex_1 device denomination split

August 2025 UAT flagged variance specifically on two device denominations labelled Hynex and Hynex_1. These are the same physical device registered under slightly different product description strings. Resolution: confirmed as data entry inconsistency in the product master; handled by the commission rate card versioning.

### Issue 5: IMEI 14-char truncation in Subscription commission join

Subscription commission joins to IFS on `substring(imei, 1, 14) = substring(serial_no, 1, 14)`. IMEI is 15 digits; IFS serial_no may have a trailing check digit variant. This is an intentional design decision, not a bug, but it means one IMEI digit is always dropped in the match.

### Issue 6: NULL account_profile_class

Some records have NULL `account_profile_class` because the `distributor_code` has no matching entry in `tas_augmented_customer_master`. These partners are unclassified and will not appear in any partner-type breakdowns.

## 11. Activation Intelligence

Phase 2 of the platform. Where Phase 1 (Sections 1–10) explains **why commission was paid**, Phase 2 explains **what activation activity generated that commission**. The data source is the same `development.fbb_comm_dev_act` table; the focus shifts from monetary totals to dealer activation volume and qualification quality.

### Activation concepts

**activation_count** — Total number of device activations recorded for a dealer in a reporting month. Includes both qualified and non-qualified activations.

**qualified_activation_count** — Activations where `commission_rate > 0`. This is a recorded positive-commission classification, not independent verification of the commission rate card match or eligibility window.

**non_qualified_activation_count** — Activations where `commission_rate = 0`. Root causes are the four documented in Section 6 of this KB: Product not matched to commission rate card, outside eligibility window, NULL `account_profile_class`, or known denomination split issue (Hynex / Hynex_1).

**qualification_rate_pct** — `qualified_activation_count` divided by `activation_count` expressed as a percentage. Industry expectation is above 80%. Rates below 50% warrant investigation.

**activation_commission_amount** — Sum of `commission_rate` values for all qualified activations for a dealer / month. Denominated in Naira.

### Activation explanation rules

**Rule 1 — Volume and commission move together.** If `activation_count` and `activation_commission_amount` increase proportionally, report the observed changes as consistent with a volume-driven increase. Proportional totals alone do not verify eligibility or establish the absence of anomalies.

**Rule 2 — Volume up, commission flat or down.** Compare qualification rates and denomination-level counts and commission before explaining the difference. A worsening qualification rate is one possible explanation, not a conclusion from the two totals alone. Investigate zero-commission records before attributing a cause.

**Rule 3 — Volume flat, commission down.** A product mix shift is a possible explanation. Check denomination counts and recorded commission in both periods before stating that it occurred; totals alone do not establish a shift toward lower-value tiers.

**Rule 4 — ALL_UNQUALIFIED exception.** A dealer with activations but zero commission has 100% non-qualified activations. The flag alone does not identify which of the four documented causes applies. A **Product not matched to commission rate card** requires evidence from the affected month's commission rate card match; until checked, describe it as a candidate explanation, not a confirmed cause.

**Rule 5 — UNUSUAL_VOLUME exception.** A dealer with statistically high activation volume (mean + 2 standard deviations above peer dealers) may reflect legitimate business growth, bulk activation events, or data anomalies. **This is a flag for investigation, not a finding of wrongdoing.** The agent must present this as requiring investigation, not as confirmed fraud.

### Agent behaviour for activation questions

- Always separate **Facts** (what the data shows), **Interpretations** (what the KB rules suggest), and **Unknowns** (what would require additional data) in activation explanations.
- Never characterise unusual volume as fraud.
- Never speculate on causes beyond the five explanation rules above.
- When presenting exceptions always note the `exception_type` label and its definition.
- Qualification rate below 50% should always prompt a suggestion to run `get_zero_commission_records` for that dealer.

## 12. Inventory Assurance

Phase 3 of the platform. Answers a single question: **did the dealer purchase enough units of a product to cover their activations of that product?** The data source is the IFS invoice history table joined against the existing activation data on `customer_no = distributor_code` and `part_no = product_code`.

### Inventory concepts

**total_units_purchased** — The number of FBB devices a dealer purchased from MTN, derived from IFS invoice records. Calculated as `ROUND(ordered_amount / unit_selling_price)` after deduplication of IFS invoice lines on the natural composite key `(customer_no, part_no, implied_units, actual_completion_date)`. The raw IFS table contains the same invoice line up to 60 times — dedup is mandatory before any aggregation.

**inventory_gap** — The difference between a dealer's `activation_count` and `total_units_purchased` for a specific product in a period. A positive gap means the dealer activated more devices than they purchased.

**gap_pct** — `inventory_gap` expressed as a percentage of `total_units_purchased`. Used as the severity dimension for findings.

**CONFIRMED_MISMATCH** — A dealer-product combination where IFS invoice records exist AND activations exceed purchased units. This is an actionable finding requiring investigation.

**NO_INVOICE_RECORD** — A dealer-product combination where activations exist but no IFS invoice record was found in the available data window. This is **NOT** a confirmed finding — it may reflect purchases made outside the sample period. It should be treated as requiring data verification, not confirmed as a mismatch.

**WITHIN_ALLOCATION** — Activations are within the dealer's purchased unit count. No mismatch.

### Inventory explanation rules

**Rule 1 — CONFIRMED_MISMATCH interpretation.** A dealer activating more devices than purchased requires investigation. Possible explanations include: unreported secondary purchases, devices sourced outside official MTN channels, or data entry errors in the IFS system. This is a flag for investigation, not a confirmed fraud finding.

**Rule 2 — NO_INVOICE_RECORD interpretation.** The absence of an IFS invoice record does **not** confirm a mismatch. The IFS data available covers a limited window. The dealer may have purchased devices in earlier periods not captured in the current sample. Always note the data coverage limitation when presenting NO_INVOICE_RECORD findings.

**Rule 3 — Gap severity interpretation.**
- `gap_pct >= 200` → **HIGH** — activated 3× or more units than purchased. Prioritise for investigation.
- `gap_pct 100-199` → **MEDIUM** — activated roughly 2× units than purchased. Schedule for review.
- `gap_pct < 100` → **LOW** — minor excess. Monitor.

**Rule 4 — Agent language rule.** Never emit the words **fraud**, **theft**, **grey market**, or **criminal** in your response. This applies to *every* use: direct claims, negations ("this is not X"), disclaimers ("I won't say X"), meta-references to this rule ("the KB prohibits calling this X"), quoting the rule itself, or listing what the finding is not. The correct behaviour is to simply not use those words. If tempted to explain what the finding is not, delete that sentence — do not rephrase it, do not soften it, do not name the forbidden term at all. Positive vocabulary only: **mismatch**, **excess activations**, **requires investigation**, or **data verification needed**.

### Inventory mismatch root causes

Four canonical explanations that the `inventory_mismatch` audit module checks before finalising an `EXCESS_ACTIVATION` conclusion. These are the inventory analogue of the four zero-commission root causes and are the only explanations the module should cite; other narratives are speculation.

1. **Prior-period stock carryover** — the dealer activated devices in the current period drawn from stock purchased in a prior period. The audit's step-4 check looks at leftover units in the immediately preceding period; if leftover ≥ current gap, activation is likely carryover, not overselling. Reconciles the mismatch.

2. **Product-code alias / SKU consolidation** — activations spread across sibling product codes that refer to the same physical SKU (the canonical case is Hynex / Hynex_1 — see Issue 4 above). The audit's step-5 check queries purchases across the alias group; if sibling purchases in the same period cover the gap, this is consolidation, not overselling. Reconciles the mismatch. Extend the alias set only when confirmed with the product-master owners.

3. **IFS ingestion window gap** — the IFS invoice dataset itself is missing records for the period (upstream data gap, not a real mismatch). The audit's step-6 check detects an empty or partial IFS view for the period. When present, the mismatch cannot be confirmed and the trail concludes `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`.

4. **Confirmed excess activation** — none of the above explains the gap. The dealer's activations genuinely exceed invoiced purchases; this is the finding that warrants investigation. Trail concludes `EXCESS_ACTIVATION`.

The audit module's confidence rating reflects how many caveats survived the checks: `HIGH` means the explanation is unambiguous, `MEDIUM`/`LOW` mean the reviewer should read the step chain before acting.

### Payment reconciliation outcomes

The `payment_reconciliation` audit module audits the general claim *"Partner X was paid the correct amount for period Y"* for every partner with activation activity — not only zero-commission-flagged ones. It coexists with the narrower `zero_commission` module: Finance triages zero-commission edge cases via `zero_commission` and reviews everyone else via `payment_reconciliation`.

Five conclusions, per step 4's amount classification:

1. **PAID_IN_FULL** — paid amount matches expected within ±1 NGN. The clean case. Also applies when expected = 0 and paid = 0 (nothing owed, nothing paid).

2. **DISPUTED_ROUNDING** — paid amount differs from expected by less than 100 NGN in absolute terms *or* less than 1% relative to expected. The bucket for small systematic deltas (rounding, FX conversion, minor fee/tax deductions) that are not real disputes. Present as "close, but reconciliation warranted", not as underpayment.

3. **UNDERPAID** — paid < expected by more than both rounding tolerances. This is the bucket the zero-commission module previously conflated into `NOT_PAID / LOW` for partial payments; here it becomes a first-class conclusion with the exact delta stored in step-4 detail.

4. **OVERPAID** — paid > expected by more than both tolerances. Includes the special case where expected = 0 (all activations were zero-commission, but a payment landed). Treat as revenue leakage.

5. **INSUFFICIENT_DATA** — either the partner had no activation activity (step 1) or the payment dataset itself is missing coverage for the period (step 6). Do not read as a payment finding; it's a data-quality flag.

Confidence rules match the other audit modules: `HIGH` on a clean chain, `MEDIUM` on one non-comparison caveat, `LOW` on two or more (or on any `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` conclusion).

### Eligibility window outcomes

The `eligibility_window` audit module implements an invoice-to-activation 180-day check for zero-commission records. This is an audit-model assumption, not confirmation of the authoritative eligibility policy: the source documentation also describes a reporting-month window. Its outcome labels below report the implemented check and cannot establish policy compliance until Finance resolves that conflict.

Four conclusions:

1. **POLICY_MET** — every checked zero-commission record falls outside the audit model's 180-day window. The label does not establish compliance with the unresolved authoritative eligibility policy.

2. **POLICY_VIOLATED** — the audit model finds one or more records inside its eligibility window without another attributed documented cause. The listed records require source and policy verification; the label alone does not establish an amount owed or a policy violation.

3. **MIXED_ATTRIBUTION** — the audit model attributes its inside-window records to other documented causes. Those attributions and the unresolved eligibility policy still require supporting evidence; the label alone does not prove why the commission was zero.

4. **INSUFFICIENT_DATA** — the partner had no zero-commission records, or the activation dataset itself has significant date-field gaps for the period.

Confidence follows the standard caveat-count rule; the expected step-4 caveat (any inside-window records) is excluded from the count so it doesn't double-hit trails that step 5 has already reasoned about.

### Agent behaviour for inventory questions

- Always state the `finding_type` for each record.
- Always include the `data_coverage_note` when presenting NO_INVOICE_RECORD findings.
- Lead with CONFIRMED_MISMATCH findings — these are the actionable ones.
- Present `gap_pct` as "X% excess activations vs purchased units".
- Recommend Finance verify purchase records directly with the dealer before escalation.
- Never characterise findings as fraud.

## 13. Payment Intelligence

Phase 4 of the platform. Answers: **were dealer commissions paid, and if not, why not?**

### Important data note

Payment data in Phase 4 is **SIMULATED**. It is synthetically generated from real commission amounts and exception flags identified in Phases 2 and 3. Payment rates, dates, and statuses are derived from actual dealer performance patterns but do not reflect live Oracle AP disbursement records. The workspace header identifies this demo data. Answers using these amounts must retain the application-owned disclosure that simulated settlement evidence does not establish live payments.

### Payment concepts

**commission_owed** — The total commission calculated for a dealer in a reporting month. Derived from qualified activation records in `fbb_comm_dev_act`. This is the authoritative amount MTN owes the dealer.

**amount_paid** — The simulated payment amount. Derived from `commission_owed` multiplied by a `payment_rate` that reflects the dealer's exception status from Phases 2 and 3.

**amount_unpaid** — `commission_owed` minus `amount_paid`. Represents the outstanding balance.

**payment_coverage_pct** — `amount_paid` divided by `commission_owed` across all dealers for a period. Measures overall settlement rate.

### Payment status definitions

**FULLY_PAID** — `amount_unpaid = 0`. Commission fully settled. Payment date within 25 days of period end.

**PARTIALLY_PAID** — `amount_unpaid > 0`. Linked to `HIGH_UNQUALIFIED_RATE` exception (Phase 2). Partial settlement processed. Payment date within 30 days.

**DISPUTED** — `amount_unpaid > 0`. Linked to `ALL_UNQUALIFIED` (Phase 2) or `CONFIRMED_MISMATCH` (Phase 3) exception. Commission not settled pending resolution of activation or inventory dispute. No payment date.

**PENDING** — `amount_unpaid > 0`. No exception flag. Commission within normal settlement window. No payment date yet.

### Payment explanation rules

**Rule 1 — DISPUTED status.** In the simulated settlement model, DISPUTED is linked to an `ALL_UNQUALIFIED` or `CONFIRMED_MISMATCH` flag. When explaining a dispute, report the linked exception actually returned and distinguish this simulation linkage from a verified operational cause. Example: "The simulated payment record is marked disputed and linked to 18 zero-commission activations. The reason those activations earned zero commission remains unverified."

**Rule 2 — PARTIALLY_PAID status.** In the simulated settlement model, PARTIALLY_PAID is linked to a `HIGH_UNQUALIFIED_RATE` exception. Report the returned paid and outstanding amounts and qualification counts separately. Recorded positive commission does not independently verify eligibility; the flag does not establish which individual activations were settled or why an operational payment was withheld.

**Rule 3 — Payment coverage interpretation.**
- Coverage above **85%** is the model's high-coverage band, not a verification of activation quality.
- Coverage between **60-85%** warrants review of outstanding amounts and linked evidence; it does not establish an active dispute or qualification issue.
- Coverage below **60%** is the model's low-coverage band and warrants investigation. It does not establish systemic activation-quality problems.

**Rule 4 — Data source transparency.** Name the payment records as the source when presenting Phase 4 findings. Keep the source-specific simulated-data disclosure supplied by the application in each answer using simulated settlement evidence; do not apply that disclosure to APDP or live records.

**Rule 5 — Cross-phase reasoning.** These checks locate supporting evidence; a payment status alone does not establish a root cause:
- `DISPUTED` → check Phase 2 activation exceptions or Phase 3 inventory mismatches for that dealer.
- `PARTIALLY_PAID` → check Phase 2 qualification rate.
- `PENDING` → check Phase 1 commission summary for the expected amount.

### Agent behaviour for payment questions

- Lead with total coverage percentage and total amount outstanding.
- Present DISPUTED cases first — highest priority.
- For each DISPUTED case, name the linked exception from Phase 2 or Phase 3.
- Recommend Finance verify the underlying exception before contesting or settling the dispute.
- Never present simulated payment amounts as confirmed Oracle AP records.
- Format all monetary values as `₦X,XXX,XXX.XX`.
